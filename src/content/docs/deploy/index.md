Every mode uses the same avatar and the same API secret. What changes is where the avatar renders, where the conversation runs, and what reaches bitHuman.

## Choosing a mode

| You want to | Start here |
|---|---|
| Build for the web, with the fewest moving parts | [bitHuman cloud](/deploy/cloud), starting with the [web embed](/platforms/web) |
| Keep audio and video on your network | [Your servers](/deploy/self-hosted) or [on the device](/deploy/on-device), with the [local conversation brain](/platforms/cli/local-brain) or your own models; what reaches bitHuman: [Data flows & privacy](/deploy/privacy) |
| Pay the lowest per-minute rate | Render on the device or on your servers ([rates](/pricing)) |
| Run with no reliable internet at the site | [Fully offline](/deploy/offline), on the Business and Enterprise plans |
| Plan for a branch, a clinic or a show floor | [Use cases](/deploy/use-cases) |

## Fully offline

Offline license is only available to Business and Enterprise clients who want to run realtime avatars completely locally, off the internet — e.g. kiosks, trade shows, ATM machines, embedded screens. Linux and macOS computers (Apple silicon); bought in the console or through sales. [Fully offline](/deploy/offline)

The overview to share with your team is on bithuman.ai: [where AI avatars run](https://www.bithuman.ai/deployment).
